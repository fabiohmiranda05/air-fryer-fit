Spinach and Tomato Egg Muffins

Fluffy, savory egg muffins studded with spinach and sweet cherry tomatoes. They bake in minutes, travel well, and make a grab-and-go breakfast on busy mornings.

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Total22 min
Air fryer350 °F / 190 °C

Estimated per serving

185calories
16 gprotein
3 gcarbs
11 gtotal fat
1 gfiber

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs (300 g)
  • 1 cup (30 g) baby spinach, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup (50 g) cherry tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup (28 g) shredded part-skim mozzarella
